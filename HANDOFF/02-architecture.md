## Data and state

- **Where content comes from:** hand-curated `src/data/movies.ts`, fact-checked
  against TMDB at author time (`npm run tmdb:audit`, `tmdb:dates`) and arbitrated
  by Buri; accepted disagreements live in `docs/tmdb-rulings.md`. Release dates
  follow a locked "first US theatrical (TMDB types 2+3), never premieres or
  festivals" policy.
- **Derived artifacts** (generated, committed): `chronology-pool.json` from
  `build:chronology-pool`; `connections-grids.json` from `build:connections-grids`.
  Verify suites read the shipped JSON, not the generator.
- **Runtime state:** React component state only. **Persisted:** localStorage
  meta-state under `matchcut:v1` (per-daily-mode `{lastSeed, streak, best}`,
  Duel `{plays, wins}` per difficulty, `seenIntro`, `seenDragPlay`,
  `lastDifficulty`). `DAILY_EPOCH = 2026-07-04` makes 2026-09-27 "day 86"
  (Buri ruled: keep).
- **Nothing a rule depends on is ever persisted.** Every deal is a pure function
  of (seed, pool).
- **Daily rollover** is the player's **local** calendar date, so the 216 cutover
  rolls around the globe over ~26 hours. Correct by design.

## Invariants and contracts

1. **The sim is the source of truth; React must match it.** Defined in
   `sim/RULESET.md`. Executable in `sim/verify.ts` (64 checks incl. parity
   mirror, conservation, casual-model sanity, full-flow tilt band ≤12pp).
2. **Same functions, not same logic.** `src/lib/` is imported by both `src/` and
   `sim/`. The sim never forks a copy.
3. **A rule, scoring, pool, seed, or pin change is never trivial.** It invalidates
   the difficulty tune and the published-daily pins; it needs its own approval,
   `npm run verify` 64/64, the other three suites, and `eval tune` re-run (CLAUDE.md).
4. **Pools are append-only and pinned.** Growing a pool must not reshuffle past
   dailies. Verify pins: `verify:solo` (2026-09-26 → 89, 2026-09-27+ → 216),
   `verify:chronology` (deal spread), `verify:connections` (365-grid digest,
   anchor 2026-07-06).
5. **Locked dependencies.** React 18 / Vite / Tailwind 4 / Framer Motion only.
   No new deps without asking.
6. **localStorage = meta only.** (CLAUDE.md "Persistence".)
7. **Quiet-phase switches** hold until Approval 5: `noindex` meta
   (`index.html:14`, pinned by `delivery-smoke.spec.ts:76`) and URL-free shares
   (`src/lib/share.ts:7`, asserted by `prod-smoke.mjs`).
8. **Security contract** — `vercel.json` must equal `security-headers.ts`
   (checked by `check:security`); CSP is `script-src 'self'`, no inline script.
9. **Bundle budgets** — menu shell ≤ 104 KiB gzip; each mode chunk lazy.
10. **Card faces are typographic** this build; card art must be scene-only if
    the art pilot ever wakes (baked art leaks year and point values).
11. **TMDB attribution** must stay visible in the rules/About sheet (free-tier
    licence, non-commercial + attribution).
12. **RULEBOOK.md tracks every mechanic change** in the same pass.
