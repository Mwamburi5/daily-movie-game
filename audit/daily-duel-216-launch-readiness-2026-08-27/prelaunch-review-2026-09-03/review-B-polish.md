## C. Not a quick win (flagged, deliberately not specified)

1. **Streak semantics.** A Connections/Solo loss increments the daily streak (`progress.ts:222`). It's documented in `RULEBOOK.md:339-340` and it's a meta-state decision, not a sim rule — but changing it changes what a streak *means* to a player who has already built one. Item 10 is the copy-only mitigation.
2. **Share text carries no URL** (`src/lib/share.ts:6`). Deliberate ("URL-in-share = launch switch" per project memory), but with launch on 09-27 a shared result gives the recipient no way to find the game. This is a switch to flip, not a bug to fix — but it needs flipping deliberately before launch, and the emoji rows are already ~5 lines long, so where the URL goes is a copy decision.
3. **Chronology tray titles truncate**: `HARRY POTTER AND THE DEATHLY HALLOWS – PAR…` in the choice ticket (measured overflow 189px into a 183px box). Today only Part 2 is in the deal so nothing is ambiguous, but a deal containing both Parts 1 (2010) and 2 (2011) would be **unsolvable by inspection** — the player couldn't tell them apart. That's a content/deal-validation question, not a CSS one.

## D. Checked and FINE (coverage evidence)

- **Zero console errors, warnings, `pageerror`s, failed requests, or ≥400 responses** across every screen, both viewports, all four modes, wins and a loss, practice variants, reduced motion, and the simulated launch day. Three separate instrumented runs, all clean.
- **Launch-day cutover works.** With the clock pinned to 2026-09-27, the menu and all three dailies load and render the new 216-pool deals with no error (`launchday-menu.png`, `launchday-solo.png`, `launchday-connections.png`).
- **Focus ring present and visible** — `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid amber; offset 2px }` plus a 6px navy ring; verified by tabbing on the menu.
- **Dialog a11y contract holds** — focus enters the dialog on mount, Tab cycles inside, Esc closes where a dismiss affordance exists, focus is restored on unmount. Verified on the rules sheet.
- **No unnamed interactive elements.** Every icon-only button on every screen has an `aria-label`. The audit found zero.
- **The 32px help / 36px restart / "New round" buttons are NOT undersized** — `.app-help-button::after` and `.daily-icon-button::before` project an invisible 44×44 hit halo. Confirmed by `elementFromPoint` hit-testing at ±21px from centre. Reporting these as small targets would have been a false positive.
- **Bundle budgets PASS** — menu shell 99.85/100 KiB gzip; every mode chunk within budget. None of the fixes above adds bytes to the menu chunk (items 5, 15, 16, 19, 20 all land in mode chunks; items 1, 4, 9 are HTML/strings).
- **Share copy lands via the async Clipboard API in all four modes**, family format consistent (`Match Cut · <Mode>` / score line / emoji row), practice correctly marked.
- **`ResultMeaning` polarity is right on all four end screens** — "Lower is better" (Solo, Chronology), "Fewer mistakes is better" (Connections), "Higher is better" (Duel). This was the thing most likely to confuse and it's handled.
- **Assets and meta are correct** — `favicon.svg` + `favicon-32.png` (32×32) + `apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180) + `social-preview.png` **1200×630**, matching the declared `og:image:width/height`. Canonical, OG, and Twitter card tags all present and consistent.
- **No stale "Marquee" brand strings in player-facing copy.** Every `marquee` hit is the intentional Duel pile term.
- **RULEBOOK does not drift from the UI** on par, four mistakes, the show-up streak, or the 216-pool cutover date.
- **200% text zoom**: the menu and the rules sheet reflow and stay scrollable and usable (`zoom200-menu.png`, `zoom200-rules.png`). Only the Solo board degrades, and that's item 11's root cause.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`** renders and plays the Solo board with no error and no layout change.

---

**Least confident:** the exact `86` divisor in item 2. I measured the break at 375 and 390 and verified the arithmetic, but I did not re-render every one of the 200+ Connections titles at the new value — a title in the 9–12-char-longest-word band could still land badly on an unusual deal, and 360px-wide devices (outside the two acceptance sizes) still break on `OPPENHEIMER` and `RESURRECTIONS` regardless. Worth a quick loop over all baked grids before shipping it.

**What Buri might be missing:** items 11, 12, 13 and 16 all fail *in the same direction* — the 375×667 layouts were tuned for content that fits, and the app degrades by covering things up rather than by making room. The 200%-zoom Solo board is the same bug as the 375 pile-card clip, which is the same instinct as the Duel compact header dropping the goal. That suggests one vertical-rhythm pass on the daily-mode shell would clear four separate P1/P2 line items at once — cheaper than four spot fixes, and it's the difference between "a small phone is a supported size" and "a small phone is where the seams show" on a launch day that will be mostly phone traffic.
