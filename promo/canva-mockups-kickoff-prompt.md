mockups"). This deliberately runs AHEAD of the Phase 0 red-pen and Phase
0-captures: it builds only what needs no real gameplay screenshots, and it
treats the un-reviewed brand sheet as provisional canon. Prompt 3 in

Prompt 3's preconditions met.

Paste the block below into a fresh session:

1. Canva housekeeping: root folder "Match Cut Promo" with a "Mockups"
   subfolder; every design lands in it. Upload brand assets via the live
   URLs (https://matchcutdaily.com/favicon.svg,
   https://matchcutdaily.com/social-preview.png). Never host repo files on
   third-party services to get URLs.

   Present each variant on a circle-crop preview (avatars crop to circles)
   plus a 48px legibility check.

   carries no movie titles and none may appear.
5. Mockup D — device-frame templates: one 1080x1080 feed and one 1080x1920

   fonts actually used, and any deviation from the brand sheet.
8. promo/phase1-mockups-checkpoint.md — contact-sheet summary, every
   judgment call, and which of the 5 open Phase-0 questions these mockups

Then STOP for Buri review. These are DIRECTIONS, not finals — expect a
